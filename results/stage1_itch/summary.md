## Assumptions

- **Data:** itch order book events, AAPL, AMZN, GOOG, INTC, MSFT; Nasdaq only.
- **Volume curve for VWAP:** PROXY: average share of day per 5-minute bucket from recent Yahoo Finance consolidated 5-minute bars (`data/processed/volume_curve_proxy_{symbol}.csv`), not the traded period's venue volume.
- **Parent order sizes:** 2%, 5%, 10% of expected volume over the 30-minute horizon; expected volume uses the same day's total Nasdaq volume (a simulator setting that no strategy sees).
- **Fees:** taker $0.0030 per share, maker rebate $0.0020 per share (config values; check them against the venue's schedule for the period).
- **Impact overlay** (a model, not a measurement): none: Y = 0.0, half-life 5 min; medium: Y = 0.5, half-life 5 min; strong: Y = 1.0, half-life 10 min. Calibrated per parent order so the VWAP schedule peaks at Y × σ_daily × √(Q / V_daily); σ_daily from each stock's training rows.
- **Resting fills:** conservative = back of the queue, cancellations never move us forward; optimistic = every cancellation at our price is assumed to be ahead of us.
- **Not modeled:** the market's reaction to our orders beyond the overlay, other venues, queue position from order IDs, auctions.
